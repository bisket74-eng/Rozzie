Grown-up Setup

Child's name

This name is used throughout the app instead of “Rozzie.”

Choose the nicest voice available on this phone.

If the child's name is “Rozzie,” the app spells it out for the voice as “Roz-zee” so it's pronounced correctly. Available voices depend on the phone — voices labeled Natural, Enhanced, Neural, or Premium usually sound the least robotic.

Record your own voice

Tap a red microphone, say the words shown, then tap stop. These recordings replace the computer voice in the matching places, and always sound the most natural.

Add family pictures

Tap the yellow picture button beside a name. Pictures are resized and stay only in this browser.
